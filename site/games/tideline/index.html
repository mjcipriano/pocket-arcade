How to sail

Start at the boat and tap a neighboring outlined square. You can sail back through open water. Each pearl bed pays its printed number once.

Each crossing costs 1 tide. Shallows (≈) cost 2. A supply buoy (⚑) gives back 3 after you cross onto it, once per chart. Reefs (▲) block passage.

Bring the required pearls to the anchor and tap Dock & bank. You can sail on for gold if you have tide left. Landing with your quota on the final tide banks automatically.
